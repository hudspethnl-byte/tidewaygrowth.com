How the Diagnostic works

  1. Week 1

    Access and data

    You give me read-only access to the ad accounts, analytics, your store and the reports you get each week, and I pull the last 90 days. I also talk with your team and each agency about how their numbers get put together.

  2. Week 2

    Lining up the numbers

    I compare what each channel claims against what the store booked, day by day, and check the tracking for things like purchases being counted twice or events that never fire. Then I write down the attribution window behind each report, since that explains a lot of the gap.

  3. Week 3

    Readout

    We go through everything together in one working session. Your agencies are welcome to join, and it usually goes better when they do. They'll be the ones making most of the fixes.

What you'll have at the end

  • One set of numbers your team and agencies all report from.
  • How big the gap is and what's causing it.
  • A prioritized list of fixes, and who owns each one.
  • A straight answer on whether you need ongoing help or can run with it from here.
