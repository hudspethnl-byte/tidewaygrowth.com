Privacy policy

Tideway Growth is my consultancy. This privacy policy covers the reporting tools I use with clients and this website.

Updated November 1, 2026

What the reporting tools read

When a client gives me access to their ad accounts on Pinterest, Meta or Google, my reporting tools read the campaign results from those accounts: spend, impressions, clicks, and the checkouts and sales each platform reports, by campaign and by day. The access is read-only, so the tools can't post, change a campaign or spend money, and they only see the accounts a client has chosen to share with me.

How the data is used

The numbers go into reports for that client and nobody else, like their dashboard, their spreadsheet and a daily email to their team. I keep them on my own computer and in the cloud services I use to store files and run the reports.

I don't sell the data or share it with anyone outside the client's team, and I don't use it for advertising.

Removing access

A client can remove my access at any time in the ad platform's settings, and can ask me to delete the data I've pulled. I'll confirm by email once it's done.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, and the fonts load from Google Fonts. If you use the contact form, what you type goes to my email through Formspree, and I keep form messages and emails so I can reply.
